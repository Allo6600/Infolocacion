Brain-Mode Map: A Tendency-Based Alternative to MBTI

Describe personality as tendencies that shift between inner-focus and task-focus brain modes, not four fixed types.

MBTI sorts people into 16 fixed types, and critics say those boxes are too rigid. This idea describes personality as tendencies that shift between two brain modes. It links those tendencies to the Default Mode Network and the Central Executive Network.
