Score Bands: Reliability and Validity of IQ-Style Tests

Turn a test score into a range, simulate retests, and quiz yourself on what reliability and validity mean.

An IQ-style score, such as one from a Wechsler-type test, looks like one exact number. Every test has some measurement error, so the honest answer is a range. Use the calculators, the simulation and the quiz to see how much the result can move and why.

How to use

  1. In Numerize, enter a score and a reliability value to see its likely range.
  2. Compare two index scores to see if their gap is bigger than error alone would explain.
  3. In Graph, move the sliders and watch simulated retests and real-world links change.
  4. In Test, answer the eight questions and read the short explanations.
