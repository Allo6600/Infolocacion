Five-Seven Squeeze: Compress Thoughts into Tanka

Squeeze a thought into the sound pattern of Japanese verse, or play rounds to fit words into 5-7-5 and 5-7-5-7-7.

Type a thought in English or Japanese, and the page squeezes it into the sound pattern of classical Japanese verse. Or play a round: write a verse for a set thought, hit the sound counts, and keep the key words.

How to use

  1. Type a thought in the box. Separate words or phrases with spaces.
  2. Press Compress to fit it into 5-7-5, or tick the tanka box for 5-7-5-7-7.
  3. Press New thought, then write each line to hit its sound count.
  4. Use every key word shown, then press Score.
