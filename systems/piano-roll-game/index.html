Piano_roll_game

A side-scrolling game you play with a piano's MIDI input, turning your notes into color, motion and speed.

Piano_roll_game is a side-scrolling game that you control by playing a piano. It reads the piano's MIDI input and links it to color, movement and speed on screen. MIDI is a standard way for electronic instruments to send note information to a computer.

What it does

You play a piano, and the game turns your playing into what happens on screen. Your notes affect the color, the motion and the speed of the game. The game has two courses.

  • Free play course: you play freely, so you can also use it to compose music.
  • Set song course: obstacles are placed to follow a song's velocity and sustain. Velocity means how hard a key is pressed. Sustain means how long a note is held. This course can also play the song back as an example.
  • Playing the set song course can turn into improvisation without you planning it.

How it works

The system has five parts. They handle MIDI input, game movement, drawing the screen, sound effects, and the song courses.

Background

The idea for this MIDI-controlled game came from the Casio MT.
