The Wexsler-ish Exam: A Parody IQ Test

Take a tiny fake IQ test, then see how scoring, hidden factors and stats tricks bend the number.

This is a playful parody of IQ-style tests. You answer six quick items and get a score on a bell curve. Then you see how the same answers can turn into very different numbers.

How to use

  1. Press Start and answer the six short items.
  2. Read your score and see where it falls on the bell curve.
  3. Move the sliders to see how sleep, nerves and practice could shift it.
  4. Try the statistics tricks to make the same answers look better or worse.
  5. Finish by tapping the things a test number cannot see.
