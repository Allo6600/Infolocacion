The Extremely Authoritative Nonsense IQ Test

A serious-looking IQ test with absurd subtests that ends by showing why a single number can't sum you up.

Take the EANIS-4½, a parody intelligence scale that copies only the format of the big professional tests: subtests, index scores, percentiles, classifications and an official report. The questions are ridiculous on purpose. Between subtests, and at the end, you get real reasons why an IQ number deserves skepticism and should never be used to judge anyone's worth.

How to use

  1. Press Begin Assessment and optionally enter a name for your official report.
  2. Answer the 14 absurd items across 7 subtests using mouse, touch or keyboard.
  3. Read the Psychometric Footnotes between subtests. They are the actual point.
  4. Reveal your Full Scale IQ, then use the experiment buttons to watch the same you get many different numbers.
