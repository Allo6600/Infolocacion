Adaptive Multisensor Perception for Cochlear Implants

Fuse several wearable sensors with deep learning so a cochlear implant adapts what it sends to the ear, moment by moment.

Cochlear implants restore access to sound, but noisy rooms, competing talkers and music remain hard for many users. This concept proposes combining several wearable sensors with compact deep-learning models so stimulation adapts to the situation and to the listener. Nothing here is a measured result: it is a design to examine and test.
