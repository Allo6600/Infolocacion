Today State: A System for Naming How You Feel Today

Today State is a simple daily check-in system that lets you pick a few words describing where you are right now. Choose one or more states from each group to build a quick snapshot of your day, then share it with your team, your family, or just your future self. No scores and no judgment, only an honest label for today.
