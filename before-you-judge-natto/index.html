Before You Judge Natto: Bold Dishes from 195 Countries

Someone just wrinkled their nose at Japan's beloved sticky, stringy natto? Take a photo tour of the world: every country has a dish that surprises outsiders and is treasured at home. Here is one wonderfully bold, acquired-taste food from each of 195 countries, proof that "strange" just means "not from your kitchen."

Algeria

Sheep's Head (Bouzellouf)

A whole sheep's head, singed, cleaned and slowly stewed or steamed with garlic, cumin and chili. Visitors are startled to see the face on the plate; Algerian families treasure it as a festive dish, especially after Eid al-Adha.

Chicken simmered with okra, garlic and thick red palm oil or palm butter. The vivid orange colour and rich, earthy oil surprise newcomers. Angolans eat it with funge, a smooth, stretchy cassava porridge, and consider it a national dish.
